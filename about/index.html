About WillToForge

WillToForge is the name I, Will Douglas, publish my apps under. I'm an independent app maker in Ireland. I design, build and support every app myself, and I answer the email.

How the apps are paid for

The main job of each app is free. Extras are a single optional payment through the app store. There are no ads, no subscriptions, and no selling or sharing of data. That is the whole business: I earn when someone finds the extras worth paying for once.

What I promise about your data

  • Your data stays on your device. The apps have no account and no sign-in unless an app truly needs one, and none do today.
  • No analytics, tracking, advertising or crash-reporting code inside the apps.
  • Nothing leaves your device unless you send it somewhere yourself, such as an export or a backup to a place you pick.
  • If that ever changes, the store's data label and the release notes will say so first, and the privacy policy will carry a new date.

For Eisenhower Matrix, every way data can leave your phone is listed in plain words on Where your data lives.

Is WillToForge a company?

No. It's one person, working as a sole trader in Ireland. I would rather tell you that plainly than look bigger than I am. Because the apps sell an optional upgrade, Google Play shows my legal name and contact details under "About the developer", as EU rules require for anyone who sells in apps.

Being small is also why your data is safer here: there is no server of mine holding it, so there is nothing for me to look at, lose or sell.
